upload.bike

CSV → TCX (in your browser)

Convert spin bike workouts from CSV to TCX format. Results can be imported into Strava, TrainingPeaks, Garmin Connect, Golden Cheetah and other fitness trackers.

All processing happens locally in your browser. Your data never leaves your device.
